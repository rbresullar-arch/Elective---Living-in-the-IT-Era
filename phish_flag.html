Is it Phish or Legit?

Your phone keeps getting messages: SMS, email and chat. Some are real. Some are phishing: fake messages made to steal your password, OTP, PIN or money.

  1. Tap a message in the Inbox to open it.
  2. Inspect link to see where the link really goes. The link text can lie. The real address cannot.
  3. Open link only if you trust it. A real link earns a bonus. A phishing link infects your phone.
  4. Decide: PHISHING or SAFE.
