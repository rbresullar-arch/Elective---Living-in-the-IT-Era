Activity 10 · E-commerce models

From one sale to a real online store.

You start with ₱500 in your e-wallet and a few things you no longer use. Grow it into an online shop that earns ₱20,000 profit. Every stage is a different e-commerce model.

C2CConsumer → ConsumerSell your used items to other people on a marketplace. Goal: ₱3,000 starting capital.
B2BBusiness → BusinessYour shop buys stock in bulk from a wholesale supplier, with minimum orders and invoices.
B2CBusiness → ConsumerSell to customers, ship orders, answer messages and keep a customer list (CRM). Restock and repeat.
  1. Read every chat, order and supplier page carefully. Some buyers, sellers and messages are scams. Most are honest; turning away honest people also costs you.
  2. Each day, finish what is waiting, then tap End day.
  3. If your cash and stock run out, the business closes and you start again from C2C.
