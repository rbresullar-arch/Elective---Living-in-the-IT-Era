Examination

Downloadable Examinations

You can take an examination in one of two ways:

  1. Online — tap the examination's name. It opens on this page and needs internet.
  2. Offline — tap the Download square at the right of the examination. The file saves to your device and works without internet.
Choose only one. Each examination accepts one attempt, and your QR code is your record.

Opening a downloaded examination

The file name ends in _exam_ITEra.html (for example, prelim_exam_ITEra.html).

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap the download button.
  2. In Chrome, tap ⋮ → Downloads.
  3. Tap the examination file. It opens in Chrome.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Install Microsoft Edge from the App Store.
  2. Open this page in Edge (not Safari), tap the download button, then tap Download.
  3. In Edge, tap ⋯ → Downloads, then tap the examination file.
  4. Do not open the file from the Files app. The examination will not work there.

Windows / Mac

  1. Click the download button. The file saves to your Downloads folder.
  2. Double-click the file, or right-click → Open with → Chrome or Edge.
Keep the file until your QR code is saved. To resume, open the same file in the same browser. Do not clear your browser data during the examination.

Terms of Use & Privacy Notice GEEL 1 · Living in the IT Era · Surigao Education Center · Effective October 2026

What this site is

The activities, reads and examinations for GEEL 1 – Living in the IT Era. It is for class use by enrolled students only.

What is kept online

  • Your class code (for example 12030-07), given when you create your account.
  • Your class schedule.
  • Your activity and examination results: scores, answers, and start and finish times.
  • A scrambled code made from your PIN. Your 6-digit PIN itself never leaves your device.
  • A backup log: the time and result of each backup.

What is not collected

No name, student ID, email, phone number, address, location, photos, contacts or device details. Any name typed in an activity or examination is removed before the online backup.

Your name

Your name is never stored online. You type it only when you make My Score QR. It goes into the QR image, which your instructor scans in class to match your class code to your name. That match is kept only on your instructor's own computer, offline.

Where it is kept and who sees it

In a Google Sheet in your instructor's Google account, protected by Google's security. Only your instructor can view it.

Why it is kept

  • To record your GEEL 1 grades.
  • To bring your scores back if your phone or browser erases them.

Your records are not shared, not sold and not used for anything else.

Who can change or delete records

Only your instructor. You and your device cannot reset or delete scores.

How long it is kept

All online records are deleted when the semester ends.

On your device

Your results are also saved in this browser. Clearing your browser data removes this copy; your class code and PIN bring it back. On a shared or borrowed device, tap Log out when you finish.

If you tick Remember my code on this device, only your class code is kept on this device to fill the code box next time. Untick it, tap × beside the code, or choose Forget my code when logging out to remove it.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may ask your instructor to show, correct or delete your records at any time.

Terms of use

  • Use your own real full name in your Score QR.
  • Keep your PIN private. Do not use another student's class code or PIN.
  • Log out of shared or borrowed devices.
  • Do your own work. Each examination accepts one attempt.
  • Do not alter scores or tamper with this site.
