Put the ideas in order

Each block is one sentence, shuffled. Drag a block by its black handle (⋮⋮) to move it, until the paragraph reads in a logical order.

  1. Practice: 3 short paragraphs. Check and retry as often as you like.
  2. Essay: 5 paragraphs about IT and ICT, 5 sentences each, 30 minutes.
  3. Press Finish when you are done. Your arrangement is final.

Activity 1 score: each sentence earns points for how close it is to its right place. The first and last sentence of a paragraph have fixed spots; the middle sentences can swap with each other.
