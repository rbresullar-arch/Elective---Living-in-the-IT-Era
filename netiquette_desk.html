You run the Comms Desk.

You are the social media and communications manager for nine students on clinical duty or OJT: BSRT, BSN, BSEE, BSCE, BSME, BSECE, BSIT, BSA and BSBA. Every message, email and post they draft lands on your desk first. Nothing goes out until you approve it.

  1. Read the draft. Check who sent it, who it is for, the channel and the time.
  2. APPROVE if it is proper for that person. REJECT if it breaks netiquette.
  3. If you reject: pick why, then pick the better version to send.
RecipientExpected tone
Superior (OJT/duty supervisor, head nurse, site engineer, audit senior, manager, program chair)Formal. Greeting, title, clear request, sign-off. Office hours.
Teacher / instructor / adviserFormal. Ma'am/Sir, Engr. or Dr., full sentences, your name and section.
Colleague (classmate, fellow intern or trainee)Friendly but professional. Clear and specific. No confidential data.
Public (Facebook)No patient, client, customer or company data, no rants, verify before sharing, ask before posting others.
