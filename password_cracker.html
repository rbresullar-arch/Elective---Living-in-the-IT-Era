Can your password survive the Vault?

You are hardening accounts against an automated attacker. Type a password, launch the attacker, and watch how fast it would fall. Clear all 6 levels by building passwords strong enough to hold.

  1. Read the level's attacker and what it beats.
  2. Type a password. The strength meter updates as you type.
  3. Launch attacker. The console shows an estimated time-to-break using real math: character set size, raised to the length, divided by the attacker's speed.
  4. Survive the estimate and you clear the level. Fall, and it shows why.
