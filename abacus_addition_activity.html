Add it up on the abacus

Numbers appear one at a time. Keep a running total on the abacus.

  1. Swipe up on a rod to add, down to subtract. Tap the top bead to add or remove 5.
  2. The blue bead is the next one that will move. When a rod is full, the next rod's bead turns orange: carry over.
  3. The next number appears only when your total is right.
  4. After 5 numbers, type the total and press Check. That is one round. There are 5 rounds.

Activity 2 score: 5 points per round, minus 1 for each wrong total you check. Maximum 25.
